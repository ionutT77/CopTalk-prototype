# CopTalk

https://cop-talk-prototype.vercel.app/login

A GenAI web app prototype designed to serve as a dedicated support network for UK patrol officers dealing with trauma and PTSD.

### The Background

UK Patrol Officers are exposed to high-stress, traumatic situations on a daily basis. The psychological toll of the job is heavy, and navigating PTSD afterward can be incredibly isolating. We started this project by asking a core question: *How can we actually help officers process this trauma?*

CopTalk is the result. We designed it as a safe, peer-to-peer social space where officers can connect, share their experiences, and find support among people who truly understand what it's like out in the field.

### The Process

Building this prototype was a massive step for my own growth, specifically in applying Design Thinking. Because we were dealing with such a sensitive mental health crisis, the project required a huge amount of empathy and user-centric problem solving. It taught me how to step back, understand the human element first, and then build the technology around those specific needs.

### Recognition

I co-created this solution alongside a great team, and we were incredibly proud to win **1st Place** at the IPS Setubal *'Technical Solutions for Healthy Development'* BIP.
